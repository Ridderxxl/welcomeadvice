DemoAbout this demo

Banco Salamanca is a fictional bank. This is its AI governance workspace: AI systems, policies, assessments, risks and tasks, all built from one case description. Click through freely; nothing can be changed. Then press Simulate a change at the top and pick one of five events, such as a new data source or a model update. You will see which review trigger fires, which records it flags, which it leaves alone, and which tasks it creates.

Limits of this demo. What each change touches is written into the scenario by hand; nothing is monitored or inferred. The regulation change is invented, not a current legal update. The classifications illustrate the case and are not legal advice. Nothing you do here is saved.

To strengthen control processes, starting with transaction monitoring in Compliance.

New uses, such as AI agents in business processes, go through intake and classification before anyone uses them.

Cites Annex III point 5(b)Assumes the system does not profile customersNo review needed
Rule
Every AI system is classified under the EU AI Act before use, and again when a review trigger fires.
Cacsuma-Detect

Microsoft Copilot
Minimal risk. Internal document search; duties for the underlying general-purpose model sit with Microsoft.

New customer data is storedNew transaction source outside camt.053No review needed

Legal name, or first and last name with prefixes. No middle names and no nationality.
Format versions
New camt versions reach all systems with a delay, sometimes months; the versions in use are tracked.
Retention
10 years after the customer relationship ends, or longer where regulation requires.

4Human oversight

No automated reporting: a second compliance officer confirms fraud before it is reported.

From 98 % alerts are escalated automatically, below 85 % closed; 85–98 % are reviewed one by one.
Lists
The AML team maintains the allow and block lists. Hits on allow-listed entities are reviewed after the transaction.
Known gaps
Bulk approve works on every hit, thresholds are vendor defaults, and name matching is not fully understood. Tracked as risks.

A full review once a year.
